This week's thesis Momentum is accelerating at nearly every layer of the stack, but the layers are moving on very different clocks. Cooling, silicon, and cloud capex are compounding now. Power delivery — both the grid-interconnection queue and the physical switchgear supply chain — is the layer stretching furthest into the future, with the first new-build nuclear and fusion capacity not arriving until 2028 at the earliest and meaningful scale not until 2030. Reading the value chain as a single timeline rather than nine separate stories is the point of this tool.

Private Markets

Automated landscape · manual diligence overlay

Each biweekly cycle, the same web search behind Public Markets also surfaces private-company activity per layer — weighted toward startup/venture press (TechCrunch, The Information, Axios Pro Rata, Crunchbase News). New companies get added; companies already tracked get their notes refreshed with fresh findings, since the pull is told what's already here and asked to search for updates on it specifically. Rows marked pending diligence haven't been reviewed by a person yet; flip a row to diligenced in private_markets.json once it has real internal notes, and the automation will never touch it again.
